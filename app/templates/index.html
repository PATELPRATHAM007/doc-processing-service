Pipeline Architecture
1. Fast Ingestion Buffer

FastAPI validates MIME types, computes a SHA-256 stream hash, buffers the document to storage, and returns an immediate 202 Accepted response.

2. Asynchronous Queue & Dedup

Celery workers consume tasks from Redis. If the document SHA-256 matches an existing completed file, cached results are returned in <5ms.

3. Gemini Multimodal OCR

The worker executes OCR with Google Gemini, preserving structural markdown, headers, and tabular layouts before writing results to PostgreSQL.
